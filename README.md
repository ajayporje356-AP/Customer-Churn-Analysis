
Customer Churn & Revenue Attrition Analysis

### 📌 Project Overview
Developed an end-to-end data analytics workflow to identify the root causes of customer attrition for a telecommunications company. By processing a dataset of 7,032 records, the objective was to quantify the financial impact of churn and transition raw data into actionable strategic recommendations.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack Used
* **Python (Pandas):** Data importing, cleaning, handling missing values, and data type conversions.
* **SQL (MySQL):** Advanced querying, aggregations, CTEs, and CASE statements to extract business logic.
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboarding, DAX measure creation, and UI/UX design.

### 💡 Key Business Insights
* **The Month-to-Month Bleed:** Month-to-month contracts account for ~87% of total lost revenue, costing the business over $120,000 monthly.
* **The Fiber Optic Vulnerability:** Within the Month-to-month segment, premium Fiber Optic users are the highest risk, making up over $100,000 of the monthly loss.
* **The 1-Year Hurdle:** Custom SQL tenure grouping revealed that over 55% of all churn happens within the very first year of service.

### 🚀 Strategic Recommendations
* Target active Month-to-month Fiber Optic users with heavily incentivized upgrades to lock them into 1-year contracts, drastically reducing the monthly revenue drain.
* Implement aggressive onboarding and customer success check-ins during the critical first 12 months to build early loyalty.
